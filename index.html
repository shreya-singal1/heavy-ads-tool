Heavy ads tool

Checks the heavy ad detection script: the script that reports when Chrome removes an ad for using too many resources (more than 4 MB of network, or 15 s of CPU in any 30 s window, or 60 s of CPU in total). Pick the kind of creative you want to test. Desktop Chrome only. Keep the tab in front and do not click inside the ad.

Creative typeWhat it isOpen
Simple bannerHTML creative in a same-origin frame, like a GAM friendly iframe. The creative runs inside the ad frame itself.Test
Cross-origin iframe bannerThe creative sits in an iframe with its own opaque origin (a data: frame) nested inside the ad frame.Test
SafeFrame-like bannerCross-origin and sandboxed (opaque origin, storage blocked), the strictest frame a creative can get. A stand-in for a Google SafeFrame.Test
VPAID video (IBV)A heavy VPAID VAST creative played by Google's IMA SDK. IMA runs the VPAID code in its own cross-origin iframe.Test
Your GAM creativeA creative served by your own Google Ad Manager ad unit. Open gam.html?unit=/NETWORK/AD_UNIT&size=300x250.Test
Any live pageA DevTools panel that picks a same-origin ad frame on any site. Shown here on Google's test ad. How to install it is on the install page.Panel demo Install

The banner pages also take any creative HTML that you paste or load from a file, with or without the sample script and the CPU burn. Beacons are captured by default (nothing is sent). Choose Send a real beacon to send one to a URL you set, and watch it in DevTools > Network.
