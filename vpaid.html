This page plays a heavy VPAID VAST creative with Google's IMA SDK. IMA runs a VPAID ad in its own iframe, which Chrome should treat as an ad. The creative floods the network (it fetches a 6 MB sample video repeatedly) until Chrome removes the ad. With the first box ticked, the heavy ad sample script is added at the top of the VPAID code and its events are posted back to this page. Do not click the ad. Keep this tab in front.
